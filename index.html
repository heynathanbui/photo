Booking a grad shoot? A few things that help

  1. Book early. Grad weeks fill up fast, and the nicest light is late in the afternoon.
  2. Pick up your gown first. That way we're not rushing straight after the ceremony.
  3. Bring something personal. Flowers, a bear, your family's áo dài. It makes the photos yours.
  4. Tell me who's coming. If family or friends are joining, we'll plan the group photos ahead.

FAQ

Questions people usually ask

Something else? Just ask me.

How far ahead should I book?

As early as you can for graduation weeks, because those days go quickly. For other shoots a week or two is usually fine, but message me anyway. Sometimes I'm free sooner.

Where can we shoot?

Anywhere around Sydney: your campus, the harbour, a park, your place. If you're not sure, I'll suggest a few spots that work for the time of day.

I'm awkward in front of the camera. Is that okay?

Completely. Most people are. I'll tell you where to stand and what to do with your hands, and we start with a few easy shots so you can warm up.

How many photos will I get, and when?

It depends on the shoot. Before we book, I'll tell you roughly how many edited photos to expect and when they'll be ready.

Can I post the photos on Instagram?

Of course, that's what they're for. A tag is always appreciated.

How much does it cost?

It depends on how long the shoot is and what you need. Send me the details through the form below and I'll get back to you with a quote.
