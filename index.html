Booking a grad shoot? A few things that help

  1. Book early. Grad weeks fill up fast, and the nicest light is late in the afternoon.
  2. Pick up your gown first. That way we're not rushing straight after the ceremony.
  3. Bring something personal. Flowers, a bear, your family's áo dài. It makes the photos yours.
  4. Tell me who's coming. If family or friends are joining, we'll plan the group photos ahead.

Places & content

Photos of your café, venue or space for your website, menu or socials.

  • Inside and outside shots

  • Sized for web and social
  • Want to use a photo from this site? Just ask

Prices depend on the shoot, so send me the details and I'll give you a quote.

What to expect

  1. BeforeYou send me the date and what you need. We agree on a place, a time and a price. For events, we go through a shot list.
  2. On the dayFor portraits and grads, I'll tell you where to stand and help with posing. At events, I mostly stay out of the way and catch what happens.
  3. AfterI go through everything, edit the best shots, and send you a link to an online gallery to download them.
