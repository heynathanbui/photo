01 / Scenery

The light does half the work.

Sydney from full sun into blue hour, the lanterns of Hội An, the karsts of Ninh Bình and a summer in Shanghai. The same view reads differently every twenty minutes, so I plan around the light, not the clock.

Good for venues and cafés, travel and tourism content, website imagery

02 / People

You, in your place.

Portrait sessions and graduation days at the University of Sydney and Macquarie. Sandstone arches, the Quad and the lawns frame the subject, so the photo says where you were as well as who you are.

Good for graduation, portraits, couples and friends, profile and personal-brand photos

03 / Events

The recap starts at the shutter.

Stage shows, a city-wide treasure hunt, a hackathon, a delegates' congress, Sports Day and Cultural Day, mostly for the Vietnamese student community in Sydney. I study marketing, so I shoot what the recap post needs: the group photo everyone tags, the candids, the speaker and sponsor frames.

Good for student societies, launches and workshops, performances, sports days, community events
